Hot wallets

Hot wallets send payouts and pay network fees for sweeps. Keys are encrypted to the worker's key. Nobody can see or export a key after it is stored. Bitcoin and Monero have no hot wallet: their payments go straight to the merchant's own wallet, so they have no payouts.

Make a hot wallet

The server makes a new key. It is never shown, so fund the wallet with amounts you can afford to keep online.

One EVM key works on every EVM network.

Import a key

Use a key that no other system signs with: Turmite counts on its own nonces.

EVM and Tron: 64 hex characters. Solana: the base58 keypair or the keypair file's byte array. TON: the 24 words, or the 32-byte seed in hex (wallet v4r2).
